# Acme Corp VPN Policy (ARCHIVED - OUTDATED)
Effective Date: 2021-01-01

## Setup Instructions
To connect to the Acme Corp VPN, download the legacy Cisco AnyConnect client from the IT portal. 

## Troubleshooting
If you get a "Connection Timeout" error, the VPN server may be down. Wait 30 minutes and try again. If the issue persists, email the network team at network-admin@acmecorp.com.
