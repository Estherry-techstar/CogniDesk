# Guest Wi-Fi Access Policy
Effective Date: 2024-01-10

## Access for Visitors
Guests can connect to the "Acme-Guest" network. The password is rotated weekly and can be obtained from the front desk receptionist or the employee hosting the guest.

## Security Warning
The Guest Wi-Fi network is isolated from the corporate network. Guests cannot access shared drives, internal servers, or printers. Employees must never share their corporate Wi-Fi credentials with guests.
