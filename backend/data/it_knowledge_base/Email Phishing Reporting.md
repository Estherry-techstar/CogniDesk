# Email Phishing Reporting Policy
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

## How to Report
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links or download attachments. Click the "Report Phishing" button in your Outlook toolbar. This sends the email to our security team.

## If You Clicked
If you accidentally clicked a link or entered credentials, immediately change your password via SSPR and call the IT Help Desk. Time is critical to prevent account compromise.
