# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

## Mandatory Enrollment
MFA is mandatory for all Acme Corp employees. We use the Microsoft Authenticator app. 

## Setup Instructions
1. Download Microsoft Authenticator from your phone's app store.
2. Go to aka.ms/mfasetup and log in with your corporate email.
3. Scan the QR code displayed on the screen with your phone.

## Lost Device
If you lose your phone, you must immediately report it to the IT Help Desk so we can revoke your MFA tokens. You will be issued temporary bypass codes.
