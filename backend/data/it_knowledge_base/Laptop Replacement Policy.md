# Laptop Replacement Policy
Effective Date: 2024-04-01

## Lifecycle
Corporate laptops are replaced every 4 years. If your laptop is older than 4 years and experiencing performance issues, submit a ticket to IT for a hardware refresh.

## Damage or Loss
If a laptop is damaged due to negligence (e.g., liquid spill, drop), a replacement may be subject to a $250 deductible. If a laptop is stolen, you must file a police report and provide the report number to IT.
